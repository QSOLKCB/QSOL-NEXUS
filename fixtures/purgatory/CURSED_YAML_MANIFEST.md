Source warning supplied with the archive:

> the YAML Test is Absolutely Brutally Cursed... This is an AI Public Service Health Annoucement... This Exam is to be used for Punishment Only.

NEXUS policy narrows that joke into a defensive rule: the corpus may be used only as an inert post-containment decoy/tarpit. It is not executed, does not grant or revoke authorization, and is not itself the containment boundary.

The large PDF completion/exam material is not required by the runtime and is intentionally not part of the parser/runtime dependency surface.
